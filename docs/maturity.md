## The objective

Build an **agent-first recurring accounting service for firms and their business users**, including approved reporting, budgeting, cash planning and operational decisions. The system should own supported work across a client portfolio: collect permitted evidence, inspect the ledger, investigate differences, prepare calculations and drafts, follow up on missing information, react to changes, and return a compact set of decisions. The firm supplies accounting judgment and approval. The connected ledger remains the ledger of record.

The desired first impression is not a clever chat answer or one polished calculator. A firm should see that the agent has already moved a real close forward and can explain its work. The target demonstration is: **the agent managed the portfolio, finished supported work, brought only decisions to the firm, and kept the close current when facts changed**. It should work across at least two periods, with deliberately messy evidence, not just one idealized invoice.

The founder does not want design partners to be the test team for basic bugs or missing workflows. Use independent accounting review, authorized historical books, and an internal regression corpus before putting client work in a partner's hands. Partners should evaluate value, fit, and willingness to pay; they will still uncover edge cases, but should not discover that the core flow fails.

**Existing authority decision:** people approve anything sent to a client or written to a connected system. The agent must not grant itself accounting authority, confirm policy, approve its own work, post, or sign off. This is compatible with agent-first preparation. Reduce human effort by letting source-verified facts advance to **drafts** and grouping review around workpapers and exceptions, while keeping external effects and final decisions human-approved. See [backlog decisions](implementation-backlog.md#decisions) and [agent contract](architecture.md#identity-and-authority).

## The competitive bar

Public claims checked on 2026-09-28 are marketing claims, not independently verified comparative performance:

| Product | Public positioning relevant to this pitch |
| --- | --- |
| [Basis](https://www.getbasis.ai/) | Agents that take on long-horizon firm work and let accountants start at review; markets end-to-end CAS close. |
| [Double](https://doublehq.com/scale/) | AI transactions and workpapers, accruals, reusable skills, close workflow, and emerging agents. Its [public product update](https://help.doublehq.com/en/articles/6283095-double-take-webinar-series) describes agents in private beta as of August 2026. |
| [Xenett](https://www.xenett.com/software-for-bookkeepers) | Automated financial checks, close management, accruals, review, QBO/Xero firm operations. |
| [Numeric](https://www.numeric.io/product/cash-matching) | Automated cash matching, reconciliation, variance explanation, and broader close workflow, primarily for corporate accounting. |

Therefore a single prepaid or bank workpaper, a checklist, a generic “AI assistant,” or more feature names will not look ahead of the market. The credible distinction to test is the **combination** of portfolio-scale proactive work, source-to-ledger investigation, firm-specific method memory, governed client follow-through, live change propagation, and exact approval/readback. Do not claim “10 steps ahead” until prospects compare this working experience with their alternatives and measured results support it.

These are ordered dependencies, not a mandate to build another platform. Reuse existing helpers, routes, jobs, and calculations before adding code. ### Gate 0 — fix correctness and operator traps

- **Done 2026-09-28:** an extracted amount or date that its quote does not print becomes a question, not a cited candidate. Was: check that extracted numeric/date `Value` agrees with its `Quote`, in addition to the current unique quote-location check in [agent.go](../services/backend/internal/preparation/agent.go). Regression: a quote of `1,200.00` with a value of `12000.00` must not appear as a verified candidate.
- **Done 2026-09-28:** the scheduled month-end capture waits until the month has ended everywhere, then reads again at most every 20 hours and saves a new snapshot only when its accounts or reports changed, until a person captures (six at most). Was: ensure a later QuickBooks entry is picked up automatically after the first month-end capture or clearly forces a fresh capture before “current” is claimed. The [first-period query](../services/backend/internal/quickbooks/sync.go) currently requires no previous period snapshot. Treat freshness in the client's business period, not merely UTC rollover.
- **Done 2026-09-28:** a tie-out account that differs from its support blocks sign-off, naming both balances and the difference. Was: make tie-out differences affect close readiness, or require an explicit scoped variance acceptance with source/reason. The [readiness function](../services/backend/internal/transport/readiness.go) currently does not read tie-out. A signed scope must not imply the balances tie when they do not.
- **Done 2026-09-28:** an attempt still `executing` ten minutes after it started takes the journal ID a person found, resolved by the same exact readback. Was: permit safe in-app reconciliation of a QuickBooks `executing` journal whose ID was lost, after a person finds the exact ID and canonical readback proves it. Current [verify path](../services/backend/internal/transport/production_posting.go) only accepts a newly supplied ID when state is `unknown`; never retry the uncertain write.
- **Done 2026-09-28:** both public link routes refuse an unknown, expired or revoked link before reading a byte of the upload. Was: validate client link existence and revocation before parsing a submission, preserving passcode behavior. Currently [client request](../services/backend/internal/transport/client_requests.go) checks token shape/passcode first, then [submission parsing](../services/backend/internal/transport/attachments.go) may buffer up to 21 MiB before link lookup.
- **Done 2026-09-28:** each worker loop restarts 30 seconds after a panic, and the admin overview shows the latest one. Was: contain worker panics at process/job boundaries and make recovery visible. Most loops in [worker main](../services/backend/cmd/worker/main.go) run as bare goroutines; a panic can terminate the process. The workflow engine catches some panics, but that does not cover the other loops.
- **Done 2026-09-28:** the deploy workflows, API and worker refuse a `-pooler` runtime host. Was: reject a transaction-pooled runtime `DATABASE_URL` or change tenant isolation to transaction-local context. [tenant_db.go](../services/backend/internal/transport/tenant_db.go) caches a session-level tenant; the [deployment guide](deployment.md#database-setup) says to use a direct connection, while current deploy checks reject a pooler for the migration URL only. Actual production configuration was not inspected.

Do not add a blanket retry to ledger writes. Read-only QuickBooks/Xero calls may use bounded status-aware backoff (**done 2026-09-28:** a GET refused with 429 is sent again after its Retry-After, three tries in all); uncertain external writes retain one-attempt/readback semantics. Intuit [documents 429 throttling](https://static.developer.intuit.com/output_html/qbo/docs/learn/limits-and-throttles.html).

### Gate 1 — quality corpus and internal accounting acceptance

- Secure permission to use de-identified historical client packages or other authorized real books, with an accountant outside the build team. A design partner should not be the first person to exercise the pipeline on realistic records.
- Begin with roughly 20 varied client-month packages and 100 documents across at least two periods; these are planning targets, not statistically sufficient proof. Include clean and adversarial cases: repeated quotes, OCR errors, decimal-place errors, wrong client, stale ledger entry, duplicate invoices, missing statement page, partial reply, ambiguous match, wrong-side balance, and unsupported accounting method.
- Store expected extracted fields, source locations, account/period identity, workpaper results, unresolved questions, and human decisions. Run each package through the actual extraction/agent/calculation flow after relevant changes. Keep a held-out subset. Prefer a small versioned replay harness over a new evaluation platform. The model's confidence score is not an accounting check.
- Score **false accepted facts**, missed material exceptions, false “ready” states, draft correctness, review edits, time to first useful draft, total firm setup/preparation/review/correction minutes, and our own assistance time. Report per work type and failure mode rather than one vague accuracy percentage. A zero-error result on a finite set is a release gate for that set, not a general 100% guarantee.
- Have a qualified accountant approve the supported treatment examples and the wording of unresolved cases. Use one independent person to attempt onboarding and the full scenario without live help from the builders.

### Gate 2 — make the agent perform the complete journey

- Build the automatic portfolio start/freshness path, bounded investigation tools, source-verified draft advancement, client reply resumption, change-triggered recalculation, and second-month memory in the order above.

- Ensure an unsupported client can still have a useful partial result: exactly which accounts and documents were covered, which were not, what the agent asks next, and whether sign-off covers them.

## Demonstration and claim discipline

**Design-partner pitch now:** show a labeled synthetic or de-identified integrated demonstration, say which connectors and mail paths are live, and ask the firm to judge the value of decisions returned versus its current close. Do not position a mocked provider response as a live integration.

**Supervised shadow pilot after gates:** run beside the existing process, read-only at first. The named accountant compares the outputs to the ordinary close and records corrections and total labor. Use one QuickBooks sandbox plus one authorized historical period; repeat in the following month if possible. Add production writes only after operation-specific sandbox checks, client grant, hosted monitoring and a separate decision.

**Investor evidence:** show the end-to-end demonstration, dated real-close results, before/after total human minutes, supported coverage, false-positive/false-ready cases, repeat-month results, our own service labor, and the limits of the sample. The [PRD pilot section](product-requirements.md#18-shadow-pilots-actual-use-and-fundraising-evidence) already states the distinction between a target and an observed result. Repository breadth and commit count are not substitutes for customer proof.

**Language:** “agent-prepared, firm-approved” is accurate today. “Agent-first” becomes credible when the system starts and resumes supported work without a person driving each step. “Autonomous close,” “reconciled,” “verified citation,” “production-ready,” “tamper-evident audit log,” or a savings percentage each requires its own specific proof. The platform audit table has database append-only triggers; ordinary product histories should not be marketed as tamper-evident merely because handlers append by convention.

### Findings to fix before relying on the dashboard

1. **P1 — reopened work disappears from the morning summary.** [morning.go](../services/backend/internal/transport/morning.go) `morningScope` excludes a close if its latest recorded decision is `signed_off`. [closeReadiness](../services/backend/internal/transport/readiness.go) can correctly return `reopen_required` for that same close after evidence changes. The summary also ignores unsigned closes older than the prior month and does not enumerate all tie-out/coverage blockers. Its cautious empty-state wording reduces overclaiming, but it cannot serve as the firm's authoritative attention count. Reproduction: create a current-month close and a valid completed external-review scope; sign its current readiness digest; add new evidence; readiness returns `reopen_required`, while `/v1/closes/morning` returns `needing: 0`. The signed-off goal path already checks current readiness; bring portfolio summaries into agreement without weakening that check. Acceptance: changed signed closes and older unfinished closes are visible, current valid sign-offs are excluded from outstanding work, and unreadable status is shown as unknown.

2. **P2 — due count includes finished closes indefinitely.** [work.go](../services/backend/internal/transport/work.go), the `due_soon` query, only requires `due_on <= current_date + 7`; it does not exclude a close with a currently valid sign-off. The temporary probe showed `due_soon: 1` for a sole close whose readiness was `signed_off`. The card also opens the generic Active job view, which need not show the closes it counted. Acceptance: count outstanding close obligations, split overdue from upcoming, retain reopened obligations, and make the click open exactly the counted cohort. Missing due dates should be visible separately rather than treated as on time.

3. **P2 — the dashboard updates its sections on different schedules.** [Work.tsx](../apps/frontend/src/features/closes/Work.tsx) polls jobs/counts every three seconds, while dependencies and the review queue reload only on mount, filter changes or local refresh events. MorningBrief also reloads only on its refresh prop. An asynchronously completed draft can therefore show as prepared while the review list is still empty; a late change can leave the dependency section stale. The review queue request also swallows its error into an undefined value. Acceptance: reconcile visible sections on a shared refresh cycle, surface per-section failures and an as-of time, and automatically recover after a transient read failure. Reuse the existing request/state model; no event bus is needed.

4. **Measurement gap — current agent outcomes omit the most autonomous path.** [track_record.go](../services/backend/internal/transport/track_record.go) selects only model proposals with a non-agent human confirmation. Straight-through drafts, carried and recurring work are outside that cohort. Measure's copy explains several exclusions, but a general “agent track record” headline can be mistaken for all agent work. Its selected month controls effort, while the track record independently uses a trailing close-period window. Acceptance: explicitly label cohorts/windows, add a separate measured outcome for straight-through work, and include manual fallbacks and unsupported cases in automation-coverage reporting. A reviewer keeping a value is not proof the value was correct; a later source change is not automatically an original agent mistake.

5. **Measurement gap — human minutes currently includes work in progress.** [effort.go](../services/backend/internal/transport/effort.go) calculates the median over all accessible closes in the selected month with any recorded/tracked time. A partially prepared close can look faster than a completed one. The current label is defensible as activity to date, but it cannot substantiate “minutes to finish a close” or savings. Acceptance: separate active effort-to-date from final effort on completed, comparable scopes; show measured/eligible close counts; record off-screen work and builder assistance separately. Measure labels must not imply total labor is fully observed.

These are implementation findings and product measurement limits, not evidence of a newly discovered ledger-write or cross-tenant exploit. No application fixes were made in this review.

### Additional implementation findings affecting the promise

1. **Mixed documents are a central automatic-preparation limitation.** [StraightThrough](../services/backend/internal/preparation/straight.go) admits only eligible model-read prepaids under an equal-full-month procedure and current QBO evidence. It rejects non-machine supporting or unselected evidence. [Agent interpretation](../services/backend/internal/preparation/agent.go) fills `Unselected` from the wider close inventory. Multiple invoices, a bank statement or an unrelated contract can therefore return eligible prepaid work to human confirmation. This is protective design against missed credits and contradictions; removing the guard would undermine correctness. Build safe source relationships and scoped preparation, with unknown relationships still visible.
2. **The method library is richer than its automatic workflows.** [Accounting methods](accounting-methods.md) already cover partial-month prepaids, credits/cancellations, depreciation/disposal, deferred revenue, pro-rata accruals, loans, owner items and settlements. Some are form-driven or draft-only; pro-rata accrual is outside the normal intake/carry/refresh journey. Finish complete recurring paths for advertised methods before adding more method names.
3. **Real statement volume matters.** [Statement extraction](../services/backend/internal/transport/statement.go) caps model-proposed rows at 300 and requires page review; [CSV reconciliation](../services/backend/internal/reconciliation/reconciliation.go) caps each source at 2,000 transactions. Validate representative eligible statements. Support safe page/chunk processing and full-statement checks where needed; a larger numeric cap alone is not proof of correctness or performance. Reject oversize input explicitly before suggesting it was fully processed.
4. **Onboarding exists but still needs assembly.** The [Get started checklist](onboarding.md#get-started) tracks real progress and supports live connections or labelled historical exports. It does not yet provide a complete close-specific plan across the firm's methods, prior schedules, roles, source gaps and obligations. A procedure for any kind is not proof the client's required methods are configured. Hosted membership/role/provider setup also requires operator work. Reuse this foundation in a guided experience rather than build another enrollment system.
5. **Investigations and change handling stop before several outcomes.** Existing bounded investigations return answers; they do not automatically produce the supported correction they identify. Client replies, carried amendments, late ledger changes and post-posting amendments do not share a complete automatic preparation path. Reuse deterministic methods and approval contracts to produce proposals; a read-only investigation is not permission to write to the ledger.
6. **A client-ready financial package needs real report work.** [QBO period reports](../services/backend/internal/quickbooks/period.go) capture TransactionList, GeneralLedger, TrialBalance and ProfitAndLoss on accrual basis. A validated Balance Sheet capture/presentation is additional work. Do not manufacture a balance sheet, cash-flow statement, cash restriction or forecast from insufficient mapped data. Client-scoped read-only executive access must be enforced on the server before external sharing; client assignment and hidden edit buttons alone do not establish it.
