# Management reports

B07 in the [agent-first build plan](agent-first-build-plan.md). This page records what is built; the historical cash-flow statement is still to come and is not claimed here.

## Management pack

A client close's **Reports** tab (`GET /v1/closes/{id}/management-pack`, MCP `management_pack`) prepares the month's management accounts from the close's newest capture, QuickBooks or Xero, whichever is newer: the same capture **Changes from last month** uses. It makes no model or provider call and estimates nothing. Everything is read from one database snapshot.

- **Profit and loss:** the month's Profit and Loss report by account. QuickBooks groups by account type (Income, Cost of Goods Sold, Expense, Other Income, Other Expense) with gross profit, operating profit and net income; Xero, whose captures carry only the class, shows income, expenses and net income.
- **Balance sheet:** the month-end Trial Balance by account type (QuickBooks) or class (Xero), on each class's normal side, with income and expense balances shown as one line, *earnings not yet closed to equity*. Total assets must equal liabilities plus equity; any difference is shown.
- **Working capital (QuickBooks):** book cash (the ledger's bank accounts, never called bank-available cash), receivables, payables, current assets and liabilities.
- **Cash flow:** not prepared yet. Balance changes alone cannot separate depreciation, assets bought on credit or borrowing from cash, so the pack says so instead of deriving one; book cash and its change are on the balance sheet. A historical cash-flow statement needs the ledger's own Statement of Cash Flows, checked against the real report in a sandbox, or an accountant-approved preparation method.

Each pack states its entity, period, basis (accrual), currency, capture time and source. Each month shown has a status: **sign-off stands** only while that close's sign-off stands over a QuickBooks capture, whose identity the signed readiness digest binds (a newer capture reopens it); a Xero capture is not bound by that digest, so it stays **provisional**, as does any unsigned month; **status unknown** when readiness cannot be read. A capture whose connection or evidence changed is marked not current, for either month. Last month's close supplies the comparison only when its newest capture is from the same ledger, company and currency; otherwise the pack says why there is no comparison. An account appears under this month's classification, or last month's when it has none this month; an account classified differently in the two months is listed. An unclassified account or a balance sheet that does not balance makes the pack **not complete**, with the amount shown. Approved drafts not yet posted are excluded.

QuickBooks account figures open their captured General Ledger rows for both months; totals are the sums of the accounts listed above them. Xero figures have no ledger-row reader yet. The agent's investigations can read the pack, as they read other close-scoped tools.

**Checks:** `TestManagementPackTiesStatementsToTheTrialBalances` works two balanced months by hand (statements, working capital, a prior-only expense, a reclassified account, an unclassified account and an untyped ledger). `TestManagementPackReadsTheCapturedReports` reads QuickBooks-shaped reports through the API and shows a sign-off covering its QuickBooks capture while a newer Xero capture stays provisional. Real-provider captures are not yet exercised.

## Budget against actual

B08. Below the pack on the **Reports** tab (`GET`/`POST /v1/closes/{id}/budget`, MCP `budget_comparison`), the client's Profit and Loss budget is compared with each month's books.

- **Import:** a CSV whose first row is `Account` and then months as `YYYY-MM` (up to 24), one row per budget line (up to 500), amounts as the Profit and Loss shows them: income and expenses both positive, cents at most, thousands separators and a dollar sign allowed, a blank month zero. Each line is mapped to the one income or expense account in the close's chart with exactly its name; a line with no match, or with two, stays unmapped and can be mapped by hand. The client's whole chart is offered, so an account with no activity yet can be chosen.
- **Versions:** every import or remapping saves a new version (migration `110_budget_versions.sql`), kept with the ledger company and currency it was made for; a version is remapped or approved only against those books. Each version shows its lines, mapped accounts and monthly income, expense and net totals before anyone approves it, and any line can be remapped into a new draft. A person with `workpapers:review` approves a version as the baseline once every line maps to a current income or expense account and no two lines share an account. An approved version never changes, so the original plan stays reproducible beside its revisions; the newest approved version of the same books that budgets the close month is the baseline. A line whose account later leaves the chart, or stops being income or expense, is named and left out of the comparison until a remapped version is approved. The agent can suggest a draft, but never approves one.
- **Comparison:** for each mapped account and each account with activity, the month's and the fiscal year-to-date actual, budget and difference (actual minus budget), the prior month's actual, and whether the difference is favorable (income at or above budget, expenses at or below). A percentage is shown only against a positive budget; zero and negative budgets have none. A difference at or above the firm's materiality amount is flagged material. Activity on an account the budget leaves out is shown as unbudgeted, never dropped; unmapped budget lines are named. Totals give income, expenses and net income.
- **Actuals:** each month's actuals are that month's close's newest capture of the same books, as the pack reads them. The fiscal year starts at the client's ledger fiscal-year start (January when the ledger does not say; when connected ledgers disagree the comparison says so instead). Year to date compares only months that both have books and a budget column, on both sides: a month without books, or without a budget column, is named and left out, so a missing month never reads as a shortfall. Accounts active only in the prior month still show. A corrected actual changes the comparison on the next capture and never the budget.

CSV files exported with a byte-order mark are read. Before this change, a ledger connection without a fiscal-year start made the close's profile say the year starts in December; it now keeps January.

**Checks:** `TestBudgetParsesMapsAndComparesByHand` (parsing, a byte-order mark, exact-name mapping, an ambiguous name, a mapping to a non-income account, favorable and unfavorable differences, no percentage against zero or negative budgets, an unbudgeted account, a month without books, a month without a budget column, an account active only last month and totals worked by hand) and `TestBudgetImportRemapApproveAndCompare` (no-budget state, an unmapped line blocking approval, remapping into a new version, an approved budget without the close month ignored, and the comparison against two months of captured books).

## Publication

A reviewer (`workpapers:review`) publishes the pack from the **Publication** section under it (B07; migration 114). Publishing is refused unless the capture is current, the pack is complete and the sign-off of every month shown stands; the reviewer writes the commentary (what moved and why, as verified) and confirms the figures they reviewed. The figures digest they reviewed must still be the pack's, or they are asked to review it again.

- **Frozen.** The published version keeps the whole pack and its commentary; rows are only ever added. PDF and CSV exports are rendered from that version, so they always agree with what was published, however the books change later.
- **Corrections are new versions.** When the books change after publication (any figure, not just a later capture time), the current version shows *Books changed: needs a correction*. A correction needs a new sign-off and a reason; it names the version it supersedes, which stays readable and exportable. An unchanged pack is not published twice.
- **Acknowledgment is separate.** Anyone who can open the close can acknowledge a version; each acknowledgment records who and when, apart from the publication itself. Sending or notifying recipients is not part of publication.

## Executive access

A client's executive (B07) signs in to the same workspace with a role that grants `executive:read`, and is assigned to their clients under **Client access** like any member. They see **Business overview** (each client's newest published pack: net income, book cash, working capital and whether they acknowledged it), **Reports** (the published versions, each readable in the page and downloadable as PDF or CSV, with the current budget comparison), **Cash plan** (the 13-week plan, labeled as a working forecast of book cash) and **My decisions** (versions waiting for their acknowledgment, which records receipt and approves nothing).

- **Enforced on the server, deny by default.** For an executive, every workspace request outside that list is refused (403): live packs, workpapers, evidence, requests, notes, the Attention inbox, search, the assistant and agent tools (which replay the same requests), settings, exports of the close and every write except acknowledging. A close of a client they are not assigned to is not found.
- **Drafts stay with the firm.** The cash plan they see counts approved schedules only and says how many drafts are left out (their amounts, vendors and notes are not sent); the budget comparison uses the approved baseline and sends no draft or earlier budget versions.
- **Agents and calculators too.** An agent signed in for an executive acts as an executive, whatever its grant, so it reaches only the same reads; the stand-alone preparation calculators refuse executives. Both rely on the role's permissions being in the sign-in token, as WorkOS issues them.
- **Their role cannot widen it.** An executive never has firm-wide client access, even if their role also grants `clients:all`, and cannot publish even if it grants `workpapers:review`. Give executives a role of their own.
- Published figures are reviewed and frozen; the cash plan and budget comparison are current views, read-only, and say so. Questions to the agent, notifications and other decisions for executives are not built yet.
