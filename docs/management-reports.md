# Management reports

B07 in the [agent-first build plan](agent-first-build-plan.md). This page records what is built.

## Management pack

A client close's **Reports** tab (`GET /v1/closes/{id}/management-pack`, MCP `management_pack`) prepares the month's management accounts from the close's newest capture, QuickBooks or Xero, whichever is newer: the same capture **Changes from last month** uses. It makes no model or provider call and estimates nothing. Everything is read from one database snapshot.

- **Profit and loss:** the month's Profit and Loss report by account. QuickBooks groups by account type (Income, Cost of Goods Sold, Expense, Other Income, Other Expense) with gross profit, operating profit and net income; Xero, whose captures carry only the class, shows income, expenses and net income.
- **Balance sheet:** the month-end Trial Balance by account type (QuickBooks) or class (Xero), on each class's normal side, with income and expense balances shown as one line, *earnings not yet closed to equity*. Total assets must equal liabilities plus equity; any difference is shown.
- **Working capital (QuickBooks):** book cash (the ledger's bank accounts, never called bank-available cash), receivables, payables, current assets and liabilities.
- **Cash flow (QuickBooks):** the ledger's own Statement of Cash Flows for the month, read at capture time alongside the other reports and kept only when it covers exactly that month in the same currency (a capture never fails for it). The pack shows its operating, investing and financing sections, the net change and beginning and ending cash, and checks it against the books: the sections must add to the net change, beginning cash plus the change must be ending cash, ending cash must be the month-end book cash (the ledger's bank accounts) and, when last month is compared, beginning cash must be last month's book cash. A statement that does not tie is shown with each difference, never adjusted. A statement the pack cannot read, and a QuickBooks capture without one (made before this), say so instead of deriving one from balance changes. A shown statement is part of the figures a publication binds; a pack without one keeps its earlier digest.
- **Cash flow (Xero):** Xero's API has no Statement of Cash Flows, so the firm imports its own for the month under the cash flow (**Import or replace the firm's statement**, `POST /v1/closes/{id}/cash-flow`; migration 129). The CSV has the header `Section,Line,Amount`, a row per line under Operating activities, Investing activities, Financing activities or Effect of exchange rate changes (outflows negative; totals are added up here, never imported), and one `Beginning cash` and one `Ending cash` row with an empty Line; amounts are plain numbers such as `-1200.50`. Anything else is refused with its row number in the file. Importing needs `close:signoff`, an open close (a signed close shows a note to reopen instead of the form) and a current Xero capture, and the importer attests that they reviewed its classification and cash scope and that it is for this company, month and currency, in whole units, not thousands. The pack checks it as it checks QuickBooks' statement, against book cash: the capture's Xero bank accounts of the bank kind. Credit cards are not cash, and any other kind of Xero bank account (PayPal, or none given) with a balance or movement in either month shown leaves the statement unavailable rather than counted or ignored. The newest import counts while the capture is of the same company and currency; it is part of the signed readiness snapshot, so a later import needs the sign-off reopened. Its source, importer and attestation are stated in the pack's note, and so in the published version and its PDF and CSV, with "Opening cash was not checked against last month's books" when last month is not compared.

Each pack states its entity, period, basis (accrual), currency, capture time and source. Each month shown has a status: **sign-off stands** only while that close's sign-off stands over the capture shown, QuickBooks or Xero, whose identity the signed readiness digest binds through ledger coverage (a newer capture reopens it); any unsigned month is **provisional**; **status unknown** when readiness cannot be read. A capture whose connection or evidence changed is marked not current, for either month. Last month's close supplies the comparison only when its newest capture is from the same ledger, company and currency; otherwise the pack says why there is no comparison. An account appears under this month's classification, or last month's when it has none this month; an account classified differently in the two months is listed. An unclassified account or a balance sheet that does not balance makes the pack **not complete**, with the amount shown. Approved drafts not yet posted are excluded.

QuickBooks account figures open their captured General Ledger rows for both months; totals are the sums of the accounts listed above them. Xero figures have no ledger-row reader yet. The agent's investigations can read the pack, as they read other close-scoped tools.

**Checks:** `TestManagementPackShowsTheLedgersCashFlowOnlyAsItTies` and `TestTheStatementOfCashFlowsIsKeptOnlyForTheMonth` (QuickBooks-shaped reports; a real sandbox statement is not yet exercised). `TestManagementPackTiesStatementsToTheTrialBalances` works two balanced months by hand (statements, working capital, a prior-only expense, a reclassified account, an unclassified account and an untyped ledger). `TestManagementPackReadsTheCapturedReports` reads QuickBooks-shaped reports through the API and shows a sign-off covering its QuickBooks capture, then a sign-off covering a Xero capture, each until a newer capture leaves the pack provisional; it then completes the Xero month with an imported statement that ties to its bank account and not its credit card, signs and publishes it, and shows a total refused by its row, a statement that does not tie with its difference, an import refused while signed off and a later import reopening the sign-off. Real-provider captures and a real Xero statement are not yet exercised.

## Budget against actual

B08. Below the pack on the **Reports** tab (`GET`/`POST /v1/closes/{id}/budget`, MCP `budget_comparison`), the client's Profit and Loss budget is compared with each month's books.

- **Import:** a CSV whose first row is `Account` and then months as `YYYY-MM` (up to 24), one row per budget line (up to 500), amounts as the Profit and Loss shows them: income and expenses both positive, cents at most, thousands separators and a dollar sign allowed, a blank month zero. Each line is mapped to the one income or expense account in the close's chart with exactly its name; a line with no match, or with two, stays unmapped and can be mapped by hand. The client's whole chart is offered, so an account with no activity yet can be chosen.
- **Versions:** every import or remapping saves a new version (migration `110_budget_versions.sql`), kept with the ledger company and currency it was made for; a version is remapped or approved only against those books. Each version shows its lines, mapped accounts and monthly income, expense and net totals before anyone approves it, and any line can be remapped into a new draft. A person with `workpapers:review` approves a version as the baseline once every line maps to a current income or expense account and no two lines share an account. An approved version never changes, so the original plan stays reproducible beside its revisions; the newest approved version of the same books that budgets the close month is the baseline, and the firm can compare with any other approved version that budgets the month instead (**Compare with**, `?baseline=`), such as the original plan beside a reforecast; a client's executive sees the newest approved one. A line whose account later leaves the chart, or stops being income or expense, is named and left out of the comparison until a remapped version is approved. The agent can suggest a draft, but never approves one.
- **Comparison:** for each mapped account and each account with activity, the month's and the fiscal year-to-date actual, budget and difference (actual minus budget), the prior month's actual, and whether the difference is favorable (income at or above budget, expenses at or below). A percentage is shown only against a positive budget; zero and negative budgets have none. A difference at or above the firm's materiality amount is flagged material. Activity on an account the budget leaves out is shown as unbudgeted, never dropped; unmapped budget lines are named. Totals give income, expenses and net income.
- **Actuals:** each month's actuals are that month's close's newest capture of the same books, as the pack reads them. The fiscal year starts at the client's ledger fiscal-year start (January when the ledger does not say; when connected ledgers disagree the comparison says so instead). Year to date compares only months that both have books and a budget column, on both sides: a month without books, or without a budget column, is named and left out, so a missing month never reads as a shortfall. Accounts active only in the prior month still show. A corrected actual changes the comparison on the next capture and never the budget.

CSV files exported with a byte-order mark are read. Before this change, a ledger connection without a fiscal-year start made the close's profile say the year starts in December; it now keeps January.

**Checks:** `TestBudgetParsesMapsAndComparesByHand` (parsing, a byte-order mark, exact-name mapping, an ambiguous name, a mapping to a non-income account, favorable and unfavorable differences, no percentage against zero or negative budgets, an unbudgeted account, a month without books, a month without a budget column, an account active only last month and totals worked by hand) and `TestBudgetImportRemapApproveAndCompare` (no-budget state, an unmapped line blocking approval, remapping into a new version, an approved budget without the close month ignored, and the comparison against two months of captured books).

## Publication

A reviewer (`workpapers:review`) publishes the pack from the **Publication** section under it (B07; migration 114). Publishing is refused unless the capture is current, the pack is complete (every account classified, every total reconciled, and a reconciled cash-flow statement: a pack without one, including a Xero pack until the firm's imported statement ties, is not complete) and the sign-off of every month shown stands; the reviewer writes the commentary (what moved and why, as verified), starting from a draft the pack writes from its own figures — net income against last month, the P&L lines that moved by at least the firm's materiality (or the five largest when none is set), and the month's change in cash when the statement of cash flows reconciles — which says what moved and leaves why to the reviewer; no model call writes it and confirms the figures they reviewed. The figures digest they reviewed must still be the pack's, or they are asked to review it again.

- **Frozen.** The published version keeps the whole pack and its commentary; rows are only ever added. PDF and CSV exports are rendered from that version, so they always agree with what was published, however the books change later.
- **Corrections are new versions.** When the books change after publication (any figure, not just a later capture time), the current version shows *Books changed: needs a correction*. When the figures are unchanged but the close's sign-off no longer stands (it was reopened, or a newer capture is not yet signed), it shows *Sign-off no longer stands*: the published version is kept as it was and is current again once the same figures are signed off. A correction needs a new sign-off and a reason; it names the version it supersedes, which stays readable and exportable. An unchanged pack is not published twice.
- **Acknowledgment is separate.** Anyone who can open the close can acknowledge a version; each acknowledgment records who and when, apart from the publication itself. Sending or notifying recipients is not part of publication.

## Executive access

A client's executive (B07) signs in to the same workspace with a role that grants `executive:read`, and is assigned to their clients under **Client access** like any member. They see **Business overview** (each client's newest published pack: net income, book cash, working capital, whether they acknowledged it, and, for the latest ended month and the current one, whether it still stands: if the books changed since, its sign-off lapsed, or it could not be checked, the version says so, as it does under My decisions; an older month's version shows its standing when opened), **Reports** (the published versions, each readable in the page and downloadable as PDF or CSV, with the current budget comparison), **Cash plan** (the 13-week plan, labeled as a working forecast of book cash, with its what-if scenarios, which they can also try) and **My decisions** (versions waiting for their acknowledgment, which records receipt and approves nothing). An executive is not given the firm's internal draft commentary, the publisher's internal ID or anyone else's acknowledgments; the budget comparison waits until the month's management pack is published and still stands (so its actuals are the published ones); and the cash plan shows payroll schedules as "Payroll", not by name.

- **Enforced on the server, deny by default.** For an executive, every workspace request outside that list is refused (403): live packs, workpapers, evidence, requests, notes, the Attention inbox, search, the assistant and agent tools (which replay the same requests), settings, exports of the close and every write except acknowledging. A close of a client they are not assigned to is not found.
- **Drafts stay with the firm.** The cash plan they see counts approved schedules only and says how many drafts are left out (their amounts, vendors and notes are not sent); the budget comparison uses the approved baseline and sends no draft or earlier budget versions.
- **Agents and calculators too.** An agent signed in for an executive acts as an executive, whatever its grant, so it reaches only the same reads; the stand-alone preparation calculators refuse executives. Both rely on the role's permissions being in the sign-in token, as WorkOS issues them.
- **Their role cannot widen it.** An executive never has firm-wide client access, even if their role also grants `clients:all`, and cannot publish even if it grants `workpapers:review`. Give executives a role of their own.
- Published figures are reviewed and frozen; the cash plan and budget comparison are current views, read-only, and say so. Questions to the agent, notifications and other decisions for executives are not built yet.
